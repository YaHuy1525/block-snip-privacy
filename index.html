Privacy Policy for Snip Stack

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Snip Stack ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile game application "Snip Stack" (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Device Information

When you use Snip Stack, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, including:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Crash and performance data
  • App version and installation date

1.2 Game Progress Data

We store your game progress, level completion, settings, and purchase status locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to our servers.

1.3 Advertising Data

Snip Stack displays advertisements through third-party advertising networks. These networks may collect information about your device to show you relevant ads, including:

  • Advertising ID (device identifier for ads)
  • App usage data (levels played, session duration)
  • Device type and operating system
  • General geographic location (country-level)

1.4 Purchase Data

If you make in-app purchases (such as removing ads), purchase transactions are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not store your payment information on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App
  • To display advertisements (including personalized ads)
  • To process in-app purchases
  • To improve game performance and user experience
  • To identify and fix bugs
  • To analyze usage patterns and optimize gameplay

3. Third-Party Services

Snip Stack uses the following third-party services:

3.1 Google AdMob

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob may use your Advertising ID to show personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads by:

  • Adjusting your device's ad settings (Android: Settings > Ads > Opt out of personalized ads)
  • Visiting: https://adssettings.google.com

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Google Play Services

The App uses Google Play Services for core functionality including billing and device identification. Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 Google Play Billing

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Google handles all payment information. We only receive confirmation of completed purchases.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share the following data with third parties:

  • Advertising partners: Device ID and app usage data for ad targeting (through Google AdMob)
  • Google Play: Purchase confirmation data for billing
  • Legal compliance: If required by law or to protect our rights

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your information. Game progress is stored locally on your device. Advertising data is transmitted securely via HTTPS.

6. Children's Privacy

Snip Stack is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Opt out of personalized ads: Adjust your device's ad settings
  • Remove ads: Purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase
  • Delete local data: Uninstall the App to delete all locally stored data
  • Access information: Contact us to ask what data we hold

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
